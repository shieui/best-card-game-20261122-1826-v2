Emberfall — Duel of the Four Winds is an original turn-based card battler: you face Gale Warden with 30 life each, a hand drawn from a 32-card deck, and four wind families — Ember for raw damage, Gale for damage and draw, Tide for healing and shields, Stone for armour and slams. Every turn refills 3 wind tokens, and playing two cards of one wind in a turn makes the second hit +2 damage (Wind Surge), so each hand is a small puzzle of costs, combos, and timing.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a desktop browser. Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it, Space ends your turn, R restarts after a win or loss, M toggles sound.
